## Which machinery, here

Employer **self-funded** plans governed by ERISA. Whether a plan is self-funded
is not visible on the ID card — HR or the SPD's funding section answers it.
Fully-insured employer plans layer state insurance law on top; this file covers
the federal floor that applies to self-funded plans.

## Appeal windows (rules and citations only — re-verify at use)

The federal claims-procedure regulation, **29 CFR 2560.503-1**, sets the floor:
claimants must get at least **180 days** to appeal an adverse benefit
determination; the plan's decision clocks vary by claim type (urgent, pre-service,
post-service) and are set out in the regulation. For non-grandfathered group
health plans, the ACA's external-review and enhanced-process rules
(**29 CFR 2590.715-2719**) apply after the internal process. Read the current
regulation text and YOUR plan's SPD before computing any date — the SPD may
grant more time, never less.

## The external review body

Federal external review (HHS-administered or accredited-IRO process, per
29 CFR 2590.715-2719) for self-funded non-grandfathered plans; decisions are
binding on the plan. The final internal denial letter must state the path and
how to file.

## The regulator

**DOL Employee Benefits Security Administration (EBSA)** — consumer assistance
line and benefits advisors; askebsa.dol.gov. EBSA inquiries get plan responses;
they are not orders to pay.

## Records rights

After an adverse determination, 29 CFR 2560.503-1(h) and (m)(8) entitle the
claimant, **free of charge on request**, to all documents, records, and
information "relevant" to the claim — including the internal rules, guidelines,
protocols, and criteria relied upon, and the identification of medical experts
consulted. Plan documents/SPD: ERISA §104(b) requires furnishing on written
request (statutory penalties for administrator delay exist — cite the current
text, don't quote a dollar figure from this file).

## Quirks

The received-vs-dated distinction matters for every window; keep envelopes.
Plans sometimes route appeals to a different address than claims — use the
denial letter's address, and keep proof of delivery.
